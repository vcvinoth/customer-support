# Refund Process

Refunds are processed automatically once a returned item passes inspection.
The customer does not need to request the refund separately from the return.

Refunds typically appear on the customer's statement within 5-7 business
days, though the exact timing depends on the customer's bank.

If a refund has not appeared after 10 business days, support should ask the
customer for their bank name and escalate to the payments team.

Orders paid by gift card are refunded as store credit, not to the original
payment method.

# Account FAQ

Customers can reset their password from the login page by selecting
"Forgot password" and following the emailed link, which expires after 1 hour.

To change the email address on an account, the customer must verify their
current email first, then confirm the new address.

Accounts can be closed on request. Closing an account does not delete past
order history, which is retained for legal and accounting purposes.

Customers cannot merge two existing accounts; support should advise them to
keep using the account with the most recent order history.
